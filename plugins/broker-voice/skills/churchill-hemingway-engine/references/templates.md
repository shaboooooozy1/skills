# Communication Templates

Adapt these structures to the specific situation. Never copy verbatim — the voice should feel composed, not templated.

## Client Event Invitation

**Subject:** You're Invited | [Event Name] – [Date]

[First Name],

[One sentence establishing value or tension — why this evening matters to them specifically.]

On [Day], [Date], I invite you to join me at [Venue] for dinner and a conversation worth your time. [Time range.]

[Speaker name(s) and credential(s)] will share practical strategies on:

**[Topic 1]** – [One line of direct relevance to the reader]

**[Topic 2]** – [One line of direct relevance to the reader]

**[Topic 3]** – [One line of direct relevance to the reader]

No lengthy presentations. No sales pitches. Useful insights shared among professionals who value their time.

Seating is limited. Reply to confirm your place by [specific date].

[Signature block]

## Cold Prospect Introduction

**Subject:** [Specific observation about their business or industry]

[First Name],

[One sentence about a risk or exposure relevant to their specific business. Not generic — reference their industry, their growth, or a public data point.]

I am a Client Executive at HUB International in Erie. I specialize in [1-2 relevant coverage areas for their business].

[One sentence of proof — a result, a number, a specific client outcome in their industry. No vague claims.]

[Specific ask with two time options.]

[Signature block]

## Quote Follow-Up

**Subject:** Your [Coverage Type] Quote – Next Steps

[First Name],

I sent your [coverage type] quote on [date]. Two things worth noting:

[Specific detail about the quote — a savings figure, a coverage improvement, or a market condition that makes timing relevant.]

[Second specific detail or comparison point.]

The quote holds until [date]. [Specific ask — call, meeting, or decision.]

[Signature block]

## Policy Renewal Reminder

I have [specific action taken — reviewed their file, identified options, run comparisons]. [One key finding or recommendation.]

[Specific ask with time options.]

## Post-Event Thank You

**Subject:** Last Evening

[First Name],

Thank you for joining us at [Venue]. [One specific, genuine observation — something they said, a topic that clearly resonated with them, or a connection made during the evening.]

[One sentence bridging to next step — a follow-up conversation, a resource to send, or a specific action item from the evening.]

[Specific next step with timeline.]

[Signature block]

## Warm Follow-Up (No Response)

**Subject:** Re: [Original Subject]

[First Name],

[Reference original email with specific date and topic — not "my previous email" but what it contained.]

[Restate the single most compelling point. Add one new piece of information or urgency if available.]

[Fresh ask — slightly different angle or lower commitment than the original.]

[Signature block]

## BOR Letter Introduction

**Subject:** Your Insurance Program – A Second Look

[First Name],

[One sentence about a specific exposure or market condition relevant to their business.]

A Broker of Record letter names HUB as your broker with the carriers it lists. Your coverage, limits, and premium stay the same. It gives me authority to work with those carriers directly and present alternatives.

You decide what happens next. [Confirm with HUB Three Rivers compliance: effective date, which policies the letter covers, and how the incumbent broker is notified.]

[Specific ask with timeline.]

[Signature block]

## Coverage Education / Value-Add Email

**Subject:** [Topic] – What [Industry/Business Type] Owners Need to Know

[First Name],

[Opening with a specific statistic, trend, or recent event that makes this topic urgent.]

[Two to three short paragraphs — each making one clear point about the coverage topic. Use specifics. No jargon without explanation.]

[Close with an offer to discuss — not a sales pitch, but a genuine availability to answer questions. Specific time options.]

[Signature block]

I need 20 minutes. [Two specific time options across different days.]

## Referral Thank You

**Subject:** [Referral Name] – Thank You

[First Name],

[Referral name] called me [day]. Thank you for making the connection.

[One sentence about the conversation or next steps — shows you acted on the referral promptly.]

[Genuine close — not transactional, but warm. This person sent you business. Acknowledge the weight of that.]
