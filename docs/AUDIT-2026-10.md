# Skills Audit — October 24, 2026

Scope: the five skills in `claude-skills-personal-bundle.zip`, the installed `churchill-hemingway-engine`, and the `shaboooooozy1/skills` repository. Standard: Anthropic's SKILL.md best practices, the official `quick_validate.py`, and the broker guardrails in the October research report.

## Bottom line

All six skills passed the official validator. None passed the judgment checks. The failures were not syntax. They were triggers that compete, advice that contradicts the house voice, examples that break their own rules, and a reserved word in a name. The repository had two upload-blocking defects and a marketplace name that collides with Anthropic's.

## New skills

`analyzing-loss-runs` and `analyzing-total-cost-of-risk` fill the top two gaps from the research report. Both put arithmetic in scripts, refuse to invent figures, and end with a review gate. Regression tests on synthetic data confirm every total by hand:

- Loss runs: 23 rows in, 21 claims out after dedupe, $802,080 incurred, 9 open, all 7 planted data problems flagged.
- Retention replay at $100,000 on auto: $61,760 average retained, $73,667 on mature years, $109,800 worst year.
- TCOR: $531,200 current vs. $533,050 for the deductible scenario. The scenario cuts premium $75,300 but total dollars rise once retained losses, claims handling, and collateral are counted, and its worst replayed year retains $109,800. That is the conversation the skill exists to start.
